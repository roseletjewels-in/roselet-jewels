3. Compliance & Financial Setup (Important for Pan-India)

Selling physical goods across state borders (from Kerala to the rest of India) carries specific regulatory requirements:

Compulsory GST Registration: Under Indian GST law, even if your turnover is under the ₹20 Lakh / ₹40 Lakh threshold, inter-state e-commerce supply of goods requires mandatory Regular GST registration from Day 1 (Composition Scheme dealers cannot sell inter-state).

HSN Code & Tax Rate: Imitation jewelry falls under HSN Code 7117, which attracts a 3% GST rate (1.5% CGST + 1.5% SGST within Kerala; 3% IGST for outside Kerala).

MSME (Udyam) & Trademark: Register for Udyam (free) to get a 50% rebate on government trademark filing fees (reducing the Class 14 trademark fee from ₹9,000 to ₹4,500). Class 14 covers jewelry and imitation ornaments.

4. E-Commerce & Pan-India Logistics Stack

Instead of managing manual WhatsApp orders for too long, set up an automated storefront early:

Storefront & Payments:

Platform: Shopify (most reliable for Meta Ad pixel tracking) or WooCommerce.

Payment Gateway: Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe PG (ensure instant UPI intent flow is enabled, as 75%+ of prepaid Indian fashion orders happen via UPI).

Shipping Aggregators (All-India Delivery):

Sign up with Shiprocket, Pickrr, or NimbusPost. They integrate directly with your website and let you compare courier rates per pin code.

Best Couriers by Region: Use Delhivery or BlueDart for Metro/North India; DTDC or India Post (Speed Post) for deep Tier-3/rural Kerala and remote pin codes.

Controlling the RTO (Return to Origin) Menace:

Cash on Delivery (COD) in fashion jewelry often sees a 25% to 35% RTO rate (customers refusing parcels at the doorstep).

Mitigation: Charge a flat ₹50–₹70 extra for COD while keeping Prepaid shipping free, and use automated WhatsApp OTP/confirmation tools (like GoKwik or Interakt) to verify COD orders before dispatching.

5. Packaging & Unit Economics

Imitation jewelry is lightweight (almost always under 500 grams, placing you in the cheapest shipping slab of ₹45–₹75 per shipment pan-India).

Damage-Proof Packaging: Ship inside rigid 3-ply corrugated boxes (approx. 4x4x2 inches) rather than soft poly-mailers to prevent bent earring posts during transit.

Inside the Box: Include a zip-lock anti-tarnish pouch (keeps moisture out), a thank-you/care instruction card, and an alcohol wipe pad for hygiene.

Pricing Formula: Aim for a 3x to 4x markup on landed product cost to comfortably absorb Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC), packaging, payment gateway fees (2%), and shipping.

    customer_id INT REFERENCES customers(id),
    items_json JSONB NOT NULL,            -- Stores SKU, Qty, Price
    subtotal NUMERIC(10,2) NOT NULL,
    cgst NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,         -- 1.5% for Kerala
    sgst NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,         -- 1.5% for Kerala
    igst NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,         -- 3.0% outside Kerala
    shipping_charge NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,
    net_total NUMERIC(10,2) NOT NULL,
    order_status VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'WhatsApp_Pending', -- WhatsApp_Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Cancelled
    payment_mode VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'Unpaid',           -- UPI, Bank, COD
    tracking_no VARCHAR(100),
    created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- 4. Accounts Ledger (Double/Single Entry Daybook for Sales, Purchases, Expenses)
CREATE TABLE accounting_ledger (
    id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
    txn_date DATE DEFAULT CURRENT_DATE,
    voucher_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- Sales, Purchase, Expense, Receipt
    account_head VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL, -- e.g., Sales A/c, Packaging Expense, Courier Charges, UPI Bank
    reference_no VARCHAR(50),          -- Links to Order No or Bill No
    debit NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,
    credit NUMERIC(10,2) DEFAULT 0,
    narration TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);
