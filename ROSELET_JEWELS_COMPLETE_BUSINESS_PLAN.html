1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity

Roselet Jewels is an omnichannel Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) fashion and demi-fine jewelry brand originating from Kerala, structured for rapid all-India retail distribution. The brand captures two massive consumer growth trends in India: everyday waterproof 18K PVD anti-tarnish stainless steel jewelry for working professionals, and micro-gold traditional Kerala temple jewelry for weddings and festive seasons (Onam, Vishu, Eid).

📖 What Does "Roselet" Mean?

Linguistically, Roselet is an English & French diminutive noun meaning “A Little Rose” or “A Delicate Rosebud” (from Rose + -let, like starlet or bracelet). In jewelry, it symbolizes petite, minimalist elegance, floral rosette cuts, and 18K rose-gold tones.

🎯 Core Value Proposition

• 100% Anti-Tarnish & Hypoallergenic: Solves the #1 customer fear of imitation jewelry turning black in humid coastal air.
• Direct WhatsApp Checkout: Zero-friction buying without payment gateway hurdles.
• Zero Monthly Software Overhead: Built on free cloud tiers.

10. Logistics, Packaging & Anti-RTO Standards

📦 Damage-Proof Packaging Specs

• Primary Layer: Airtight heavy-gauge anti-tarnish ziplock pouch (protects against Kerala moisture).
• Secondary Layer: Custom velvet pouch or branded micro-drawer box with gold hot-foil logo.
• Outer Shipper: Rigid 3-ply corrugated box ($4 imes 4 imes 2$ inches). Never use soft polybags.
• Inserts: Jewelry care instruction card, microfiber cloth, and printed GST Tax Invoice.

🚚 Logistics & Courier Partners

• Aggregator: Shiprocket or NimbusPost.
• Metro & Express: Delhivery Air or BlueDart (2–4 days pan-India).
• Deep Rural & Kerala Pin Codes: DTDC Surface or India Post Speed Post.
• Shipping Slabs: Lightweight package (under 500g) qualifies for lowest courier slab (₹45–₹65 pan-India).

⚠️ Cash-on-Delivery (COD) RTO Control Protocol

In Indian fashion jewelry, uncontrolled COD sees 25%–35% parcels rejected at the doorstep. Roselet Jewels enforces two rules: (1) Free shipping on all prepaid UPI orders, with a flat ₹50–₹70 surcharge on COD orders. (2) Mandatory automated WhatsApp confirmation message before dispatching any COD box.
