1. Executive Summary & Business Model

Roselet Jewels is positioned to capture the high-margin anti-tarnish (18K PVD stainless steel) and Kerala festive imitation jewelry market with pan-India delivery. Instead of paying heavy monthly subscription fees to platforms like Shopify (₹5,000–₹8,000/mo) or losing 2%–3% per transaction to payment gateways during launch, we are deploying a 100% Cloud-Based Custom Storefront & Back-Office ERP. Customers browse our catalog online and complete their order via an automated WhatsApp bridge, while partners manage live inventory, GST invoicing, accounting, and Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp chats from one unified cloud dashboard.

2. End-to-End Order & Accounting Workflow

STEP 1

Customer Browses Site

Clicks link from Instagram bio or ads. Selects ornaments on roseletjewels.com & enters delivery PIN code.

STEP 2

Cloud Order Capture

System saves Customer Profile & creates Order #RJ-1001 in Cloud DB before opening WhatsApp.

STEP 3

Direct UPI / Chat

Customer sends pre-typed WhatsApp message. Admin shares UPI QR code (0% gateway fee) or confirms COD.

STEP 4

1-Click Bill & Stock

Admin clicks Confirm: Stock deducts automatically, 3% GST posts to Ledger, & PDF Invoice is generated.

5. Core Modules & Partner Benefits

System Module Operational Advantage for Partners
1. Live Stock & Reorder Alert Tracks exact purchase cost, selling price, and stock count per SKU. Automatically hides sold-out designs on the website and flags items dropping below 3 units.
2. Unified Social Inbox (IG + FB + WhatsApp) Embeds Chatwoot Cloud so messages from Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp appear in one dashboard. Also supports 10-second manual entry for direct DM orders.
3. Accounting & Real Profit Engine Records stock purchases, packaging boxes, courier bills, and Meta Ad spend. Shows live Net Profit = Total Revenue – Product Cost (COGS) – Operating Expenses.
4. Customer 360° CRM Asset Builds a permanent, searchable phonebook of every buyer and lead across India for repeat festive WhatsApp broadcasts (Onam, Vishu, Eid, Wedding Season).
